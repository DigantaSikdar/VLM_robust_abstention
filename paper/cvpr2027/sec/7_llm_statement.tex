\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
We used an LLM-based assistant to generate figures from the authors' designs and data, to
check the manuscript for consistent terminology and notation, to fix typesetting errors, and
to check bibliography entries against their arXiv and venue pages. All research ideas,
experiments, analyses and conclusions are the authors' own, and the authors reviewed every
change.
